Privacy Policy

NoteHax - Smart Hacker Notebook

Effective Date: February 2024

Developer: BYRON AGRO INDIA PRIVATE LIMITED

This Privacy Policy explains how NoteHax ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

NoteHax is designed as an offline-first note-taking application. The information you provide includes:

  • Notes Content: Text, images, and any other content you create or add to your notes
  • Tags and Categories: Hashtags and organizational metadata you use
  • Settings and Preferences: Your app customization choices
  • Security Settings: PIN or biometric authentication preferences (stored locally)

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

The app may collect certain information automatically:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version
  • App Usage Data: Features used, frequency of use (stored locally only)
  • Crash Reports: Error logs to improve app stability (if you opt-in)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Provide Core Functionality: Enable note creation, editing, and management
  • Local Storage: Store your notes securely on your device
  • Smart Features: Auto-detect phone numbers, emails, URLs, and hashtags in your notes
  • Security Features: Protect locked notes with encryption, PIN, or biometric authentication
  • Backup and Restore: Enable local backup and restore of your data
  • App Improvement: Understand app usage to improve features and fix bugs

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Local Storage

All your notes and personal data are stored locally on your device. We do not transmit, upload, or store your notes on any external servers or cloud services.

3.2 Data Security Measures

  • Encryption: Locked notes are encrypted using AES-256 encryption
  • Secure Storage: Sensitive information is stored using Flutter Secure Storage
  • Biometric Protection: Support for fingerprint and face recognition
  • PIN Protection: Optional PIN lock for secure access

3.3 Backup Security

When you create a backup, the data is exported as a JSON file on your device. This file contains your notes in plain text (except locked notes which remain encrypted). You are responsible for securing this backup file.

4. Permissions Required

NoteHax requires the following permissions to function properly:

  • Camera: To scan text using OCR (Optical Character Recognition) feature
  • Microphone: To enable voice-to-text note creation
  • Storage: To read/write notes and backup files on your device
  • Biometric/Fingerprint: To secure locked notes with biometric authentication
  • Internet: For opening URLs detected in notes (no data transmission)

Note: All permissions are optional and only requested when you use the specific features that require them. You can deny permissions and still use the core note-taking features.

5. Third-Party Services

NoteHax is designed to work completely offline. We do not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app does not:

  • Send data to external servers
  • Display advertisements
  • Track your behavior across apps or websites
  • Share your information with third parties

6. Children's Privacy

NoteHax is suitable for users of all ages. Since the app does not collect or transmit personal information to external servers, it complies with children's privacy protection requirements. However, we recommend parental supervision for users under 13 years of age.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained on your device as long as:

  • You keep the app installed
  • You don't manually delete your notes
  • You don't clear the app's data

To completely delete your data:

  • Delete notes individually from within the app
  • Uninstall the app from your device
  • Clear app data from your device's settings

8. Your Rights and Choices

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: You can access all your notes at any time through the app
  • Modification: Edit or update your notes whenever you want
  • Deletion: Delete individual notes or all data at once
  • Export: Create backups to transfer your data
  • Opt-out: Deny permissions for features you don't want to use

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any material changes by:

  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Posting the new policy on this page
  • Providing an in-app notification (for significant changes)

Your continued use of the app after any changes indicates your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

10. International Data Transfers

Since NoteHax stores all data locally on your device and does not transmit data to external servers, there are no international data transfers involved.
