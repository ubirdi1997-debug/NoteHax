# Screenshot Requirements for Google Play Console

## Required Screenshots

Google Play Console requires **at least 2 screenshots** but recommends **4-8 screenshots** for better conversion.

### Screenshot Specifications

#### Phone Screenshots
- **Required**: 2-8 screenshots
- **Format**: PNG or JPEG
- **Dimensions**: 
  - Minimum: 320px
  - Maximum: 3840px
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 or 9:16 recommended
- **Recommended Size**: 1080x1920 (Portrait) or 1920x1080 (Landscape)

#### Tablet Screenshots (Optional but Recommended)
- **Number**: 1-8 screenshots
- **Format**: PNG or JPEG
- **Dimensions**:
  - Minimum: 320px
  - Maximum: 3840px
- **Recommended Size**: 1600x2560 (Portrait) or 2560x1600 (Landscape)

## Feature Graphic (Required)

### Specifications
- **Dimensions**: 1024 x 500 pixels (exactly)
- **Format**: PNG or JPEG
- **File Size**: Max 1MB
- **Content**: 
  - App icon/logo
  - App name "NoteHax"
  - Tagline: "Smart Hacker Notebook"
  - Key features (3-4 bullet points)
  - Neon blue/green color scheme

## Promotional Graphics (Optional but Recommended)

### Promo Graphic
- **Dimensions**: 180 x 120 pixels
- **Format**: PNG or JPEG
- Use a simplified version of feature graphic

### TV Banner (if supporting Android TV)
- **Dimensions**: 1280 x 720 pixels
- **Format**: PNG or JPEG

## Screenshot Design Tips

1. **Use Device Frames**: Show screenshots in realistic device frames
2. **Add Captions**: Short, descriptive text overlay on screenshots
3. **Highlight Features**: Use arrows or circles to draw attention
4. **Show Real Content**: Use actual notes, not Lorem Ipsum
5. **Consistent Style**: Use same color scheme as app
6. **Clear Text**: Ensure all text is readable
7. **Show Benefits**: Focus on what users can do
8. **Progressive Disclosure**: Start with simple, end with advanced features

## Tools for Creating Screenshots
